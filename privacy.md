# BrowseSwitch Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026. BrowseSwitch was previously named Chrome Windows Switcher.

## Information handled on your Mac

BrowseSwitch reads the titles, identifiers, and minimized state of your open Safari and Google Chrome windows to show a searchable picker. When you choose a window, it asks that browser to bring the window forward and, if necessary, restore it.

Window information and search text are processed in memory on your Mac and are not sent to the developer. The app does not read page contents, browsing history, cookies, or passwords. Search text and window titles are not saved as search history.

The app stores its own preferences locally, including your keyboard shortcut, sorting choice, and per-browser inclusion settings. macOS manages launch-at-login and Automation permission settings. You can change these through the app's Preferences and macOS System Settings.

## Collection, tracking, and sharing

The app has no account service, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or cloud backend. It does not collect or transmit your window titles, searches, or preferences to the developer or other third parties.

Apple may handle App Store transactions, diagnostics, and TestFlight feedback under Apple's own privacy terms and the choices you make in Apple's services. Safari and Google Chrome operate under their own privacy terms. This policy describes BrowseSwitch, not those separate services.

## Permission and control

The app asks for Automation permission separately for enabled, running browsers (Safari and Google Chrome) because listing and switching their windows is its core function. You can revoke access in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation**; switching windows for that browser will be unavailable until you grant it again. Other authorized browsers remain usable.

The app does not request access to all files, contacts, microphone, camera, or location.

## Support

For support, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/viktorkelemen/chrome-windows-switcher-support/issues/new). Share only the information necessary to describe the problem, and avoid including private window titles, screenshots, credentials, or other personal information. Information you voluntarily post in a support request is separate from data processed by the app and is governed by GitHub's terms and privacy policy.

## Changes

This policy will be updated before a release that changes how the app handles information. The effective date above identifies the current version.
